INTERSTELLAR EXPANSION CAMPAIGN v1.3
===================================

For KSP 1.12.5 with Contract Configurator, Kerbalism, Promised Worlds and the
parts/mods used by the supplied craft.  Plock also requires the user's OPM setup.

DISCOVERY / PROGRESSION
-----------------------
Tuun:
  Reach/discover Tuun -> build Puf Forward Operations Station.

Debdeb:
  Reach/discover Debdeb -> BOTH salvage missions become eligible:
    - Gurdamma Sentinel Station rescue/salvage
    - Donk Frontier Base rescue/salvage
  Complete both -> Gurdamma Station -> Gurdamma Base -> Lapat Station ->
  Lapat Base -> Umod Station -> Umod Base.

Independent late-game mystery events:
  Reach Plock -> investigate/salvage Plock Echo Probe.
  Reach Jool -> investigate/salvage Bop Leviathan.

INTEGRATED SALVAGE ASSETS
-------------------------
Gurdamma Sentinel Station
  91 parts, Active Shield, 85% passive shielding.
  Emergency: four solar arrays + one relay failed; depleted power/consumables.
  Crew: 4.

Donk Frontier Base
  92 parts, 80% passive shielding.
  Emergency: three relays, two solar arrays, and greenhouse controller failed;
  depleted power, food, water, oxygen, and nitrogen.
  Crew: 3, spawned outside the base.

Plock Echo Probe
  16 parts, unmanned.
  Emergency: zero EC; attitude control, all communications and all solar arrays
  failed.  Close rendezvous completes the investigation; ownership transfers.

Bop Leviathan
  102 parts, unmanned mothership, Active Shield, 80% passive shielding.
  Emergency: attitude controller, Active Shield, reactor controller, long-range
  relay, and four RCS units failed; batteries at 2%.
  Docking secures the mothership and transfers ownership.

PUF BUILD REQUIREMENT
---------------------
The Tuun station remains intentionally compact: <=80 t, 4-6 seats, >=2 crew,
relay communications, docking, laboratory capability, an Active Shield, and
Kerbalism life-support/shielding capacity.

v1.3 Donk command-root correction
----------------------------------
Donk Frontier Base was rerooted onto its SSPX 5 m command core. Surface spawn now uses neutral pitch/roll and 1 m clearance.
