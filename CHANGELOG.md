- Combined the existing Agency Station & Surface Rescue **v2.27** and Interstellar Expansion Campaign **v1.3** sources into one installable package.

- Added static validation and a SHA-256 manifest.

Earlier component histories are recorded in the two campaign `GameData/*/README.txt` files.
